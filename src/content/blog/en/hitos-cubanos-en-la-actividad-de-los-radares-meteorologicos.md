<p class="wp-block-paragraph" style="font-size:clamp(14px, 0.875rem + ((1vw - 3.2px) * 0.392), 18px);line-height:1.7"><em><b><strong>Let's be realistic </strong>—</b></em><strong><em> let's do the impossible!          Ernesto Che Guevara</em></strong></p>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph">Since 1983, Cuban weather radars have undergone a constant and growing process of technological innovation. Here are the main milestones driven by the Laboratorio de Desarrollo Técnico (LADETEC, Technical Development Laboratory).</p>



<!--more-->



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1983</strong>.<strong> &nbsp; </strong>A novel DIGITAL signal processor for radar was brought online. The first of its kind in Cuba (including weather and military radars), and also novel for the meteorological system in the Soviet Union. It was called the Meteorological Objects Processing Block.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_040.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_040.jpg?w=534" alt="" class="wp-image-4626" width="670" height="670" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_040.jpg 534w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_040-300x300.jpg 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_040-150x150.jpg 150w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 670px) 100vw, 670px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1988</strong>. &nbsp; A PC-based system for processing weather radar data, with digital data transmission, came into operation. It was the first in the world to use a PC (IBM-XT compatible) for this purpose. Designed and built by Cuban personnel. It was called SISTEMA HERMES (HERMES SYSTEM).</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_037.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_037.jpg?w=614" alt="" class="wp-image-4669" width="670" height="670" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_037.jpg 614w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_037-300x300.jpg 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/img_20200617_094652_037-150x150.jpg 150w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 670px) 100vw, 670px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1990</strong>. &nbsp; The Laboratorio de Desarrollo Técnico (LADETEC) was created by Dra. Rosa Elena Simeón Negrín, at the time president of the ACC (Academia de Ciencias de Cuba, Cuban Academy of Sciences), headquartered in Camagüey, to focus on technological innovation with national reach.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/camag2.jpg"><img decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/camag2.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-4660" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1992</strong>. &nbsp; An automatic controller for the full operation of a weather radar came into operation. It was installed at the Camagüey radar and was the first of its kind in Cuba to perform every action needed to operate a weather radar. It was called OPERADOR AUTOMÁTICO (AUTOMATIC OPERATOR).</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/opermrl5.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="768" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/opermrl5.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-4671" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/opermrl5.jpg 1024w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/opermrl5-300x225.jpg 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/opermrl5-768x576.jpg 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></a></figure><p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1992</strong>. &nbsp; A PC-based system for computerized reception of radio facsimile signals went into operation. It was built for the Coastal Ecosystems Center of Cayo Coco. It was the first of its kind in Cuba. It was called MICROFAX.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-22.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-22.png?w=300" alt="" class="wp-image-4673" width="670" height="375" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1993</strong>. &nbsp; A system went into operation to acquire signals, process volumetric data, and control all operations of a weather radar, running automatic control simultaneously with manual control, on the MRL-5 radar in Camagüey. It was the first device of its kind in Cuba that allowed fully automatic operation. It was called ARGOS.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-20.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="954" height="735" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-20.png?w=954" alt="" class="wp-image-4643" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-20.png 954w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-20-300x231.png 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-20-768x592.png 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 954px) 100vw, 954px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1993</strong>. The LADETEC team, together with staff from the Active Influence Department of the Camagüey Meteorological Center, won the Academy of Sciences Award and the State Council Special Award for the work titled Automated Radar Complex for Rain Enhancement (CRAILL).</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-23.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1033" height="720" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-23.png?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-4683" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-23.png 1033w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-23-300x209.png 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-23-1024x714.png 1024w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-23-768x535.png 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1033px) 100vw, 1033px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1997</strong>. &nbsp; For the first time in Cuba, a weather radar (a new MRL-5) was assembled without the manufacturer's involvement. It was installed at Loma de la Mula, in Camagüey. This was the first MRL-5 radar in the world (307 units were built, 67 of them exported to other countries) to have its conventional controls removed, leaving only computerized automatic control.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/inaguracionloma.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="931" height="631" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/inaguracionloma.png?w=931" alt="" class="wp-image-4655" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/inaguracionloma.png 931w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/inaguracionloma-300x203.png 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/inaguracionloma-768x521.png 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 931px) 100vw, 931px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1998</strong>.   For the first time, digital signals from a weather radar (the MRL-5M in Camagüey) were sent to the U.S. Hurricane Center. It happened as Hurricane Georges passed.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/georges111.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/georges111.png?w=662" alt="" class="wp-image-4689" width="671" height="619" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/georges111.png 662w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/georges111-300x277.png 300w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 671px) 100vw, 671px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1998</strong>. &nbsp; The Technical Development Laboratory (LADETEC) received the Outstanding Award at the 12th National Science and Technology Forum for the paper titled MRL-5M: Automatic Weather Radar.</p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/camaguey.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/camaguey.jpg?w=640" alt="" class="wp-image-4685" width="670" height="503" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/camaguey.jpg 640w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/camaguey-300x225.jpg 300w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 670px) 100vw, 670px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2000</strong>.&nbsp; The first MRL-5M radar in Cuba went into operation at Pico San Juan (Cienfuegos), with automatic operation remotely controllable from any point on the Instituto de Meteorología's corporate network. Industrial prototype for the modernization of the MRL-5 radars.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/f62.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/f62.jpg?w=640" alt="" class="wp-image-4662" width="668" height="501" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/f62.jpg 640w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/f62-300x225.jpg 300w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 668px) 100vw, 668px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2001</strong>.  For the first time in Cuba, the digital signals from 3 automated weather radars (Camagüey, Pico San Juan, and Casablanca) went up on the Internet. It happened as Hurricane Michelle passed.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-24.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-24.png?w=241" alt="" class="wp-image-4687" width="669" height="580" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2002</strong>.&nbsp; The first RC-32 BM radar in Cuba went into operation at Punta del Este (Isla de la Juventud), with automatic operation remotely controllable from any point on the Instituto de Meteorología's corporate network.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/pde_bandera.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="840" height="577" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/pde_bandera.jpg?w=840" alt="" class="wp-image-4658" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/pde_bandera.jpg 840w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/pde_bandera-300x206.jpg 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/pde_bandera-768x528.jpg 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 840px) 100vw, 840px" /></a></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2003</strong>. &nbsp; The Centro Nacional de Radares (National Radar Center) was created, headquartered in Camagüey.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-19.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1188" height="541" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-19.png?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-4635" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-19.png 1188w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-19-300x137.png 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-19-1024x466.png 1024w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-19-768x350.png 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1188px) 100vw, 1188px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2005</strong>. &nbsp; Cuba's first Doppler radar (METEOR 1500S) was installed in record time (2 months) on the hill El Paraíso (Holguín). The installation was carried out by engineers from the Centro de Radares, with no involvement from the manufacturer — the second time in Cuba a radar had been installed using our own resources. This radar was loaned by Venezuela for cloud-seeding operations in eastern Cuba.</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsc03640.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsc03640.jpg?w=640" alt="" class="wp-image-4630" width="669" height="502" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsc03640.jpg 640w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsc03640-300x225.jpg 300w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 669px) 100vw, 669px" /></a></figure><p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2006</strong>. &nbsp; The generalized modernization, computerization and automation of the Instituto de Meteorología (Cuba's national weather institute)'s seven radars was completed. This enabled automatic, remote command of all of them from any point in the network, and also enabled instant transmission of the processed information to a corporate website — both for each radar individually and as the Mosaic of all of them at once.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/autom_historia.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="944" height="507" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/autom_historia.jpg?w=944" alt="" class="wp-image-4641" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/autom_historia.jpg 944w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/autom_historia-300x161.jpg 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/autom_historia-768x412.jpg 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 944px) 100vw, 944px" /></a></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2006</strong>. &nbsp; The engineering team of the Centro de Radares (LADETEC) was awarded the Orden Finlay (one of Cuba's top state honors).</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsci0008.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="2048" height="1536" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsci0008.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-4639" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsci0008.jpg 2048w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsci0008-300x225.jpg 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsci0008-1024x768.jpg 1024w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsci0008-768x576.jpg 768w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsci0008-1536x1152.jpg 1536w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 2048px) 100vw, 2048px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2006</strong>. &nbsp; The engineering team of the Centro de Radares (LADETEC) won the Distinción Relevante (Outstanding Distinction) award for their paper at the XV National Science and Technology Forum.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/14_02-003.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="768" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/14_02-003.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-4637" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/14_02-003.jpg 1024w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/14_02-003-300x225.jpg 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/14_02-003-768x576.jpg 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2007</strong>.   The engineering team of the Centro de Radares (LADETEC) won the National Award for Technological Innovation and the CITMA (Cuba's Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment) award for the work with the greatest environmental contribution.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-25.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="676" height="808" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-25.png?w=676" alt="" class="wp-image-4692" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-25.png 676w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/image-25-251x300.png 251w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 676px) 100vw, 676px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2007</strong>. &nbsp; Commercial export of the Centro de Radares' specialists' services to Venezuela began.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/vesta.venezuela.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1000" height="583" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/vesta.venezuela.jpg?w=1000" alt="" class="wp-image-4633" srcset="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/vesta.venezuela.jpg 1000w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/vesta.venezuela-300x175.jpg 300w, https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/vesta.venezuela-768x448.jpg 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1000px) 100vw, 1000px" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2008</strong>. &nbsp; The passage of the devastating hurricanes Gustav and Ike was detected and tracked with INSMET's 8 operational weather radars, with digital signal transmission to all of Cuba and the rest of the world via the Internet.</p><p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2010</strong>. &nbsp; The free ORPG software, used in the 159 WSR-88D radars of the U.S. National Weather Service, was assimilated in Cuba. This was the first time that a service radar outside the U.S. achieved the assimilation of this software.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsc01093-2.jpg"><img decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/dsc01093-2.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-4653" /></a></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2011</strong>. &nbsp; Commercial export of the Radar Center's specialists' services to Colombia began.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/100_0286.jpg"><img decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/100_0286.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-4675" /></a></figure>



<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2012</strong>. &nbsp; The prototype of the Cuban Doppler radar, the RD-200SX, was put into operation in Camagüey — just in time to catch Tropical Storm Isaac and Hurricane Sandy with it, fulfilling the task set by the Commander in Chief that launched the Battle of Ideas.</p>

<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2014</strong>. &nbsp; Commercial export of the Radar Center's specialists' services to Nicaragua began.</p>

<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2015</strong>. &nbsp; The TITAN software, developed by the U.S. National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR), was assimilated for the first time in Cuba, being installed in Camagüey and Casablanca, and later on Isla de la Juventud.</p>

<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2015</strong>. &nbsp; LADETEC engineers took part in the installation of a radar in Nicaragua, providing advisory support for a year.</p>

<p class="has-text-align-justify wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2017</strong>.&nbsp; The METEOR 1500S radar in Holguín was brought online with the Cuban radar control system, modifying the VESTA|RDA-M software (M for Modified) to interface with this radar's original PROFIBUS control modules.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><a href="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/20180918_102309.jpg"><img decoding="async" src="https://blog-media.ladetec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/20180918_102309.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-4681" /></a></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2018</strong>.&nbsp; The MRL-5M radar (Soviet-built weather radar) at Casa Blanca was modernized, partially converting it into the prototype of the RD-100S radar, incorporating all the new developments aimed at the Cuban Doppler and Polarimetric radar.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2020</strong>.&nbsp; The RD-100S radar was completed as the first series-produced unit of the Cuban Weather Radar (Doppler), in operation since November 11, 2020 at Loma de Casa Blanca, on the grounds of the Instituto de Meteorologia (Cuban Institute of Meteorology).</p>
